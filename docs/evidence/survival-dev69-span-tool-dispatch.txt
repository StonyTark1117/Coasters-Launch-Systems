This is the controller binder's geometry guard, proving this click also reached SpanToolItem.useOn instead of the controller's former status interaction.

The temporary rail deliberately used a manually connected curved native track and therefore did not satisfy the production straight/level geometry gate. This record demonstrates player dispatch to both binders, not a successful installation or a complete ride.
